Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy describes how "The Clip Feed" publishing tool (the "App") handles information. The App is operated by Vasco Ribeiro, Spain ("we", "us"). Contact: bettencourt.ribeiro@gmail.com.

1. What the App does

The App is a private tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to The Clip Feed's own TikTok and YouTube accounts using the official TikTok and YouTube (Google) APIs. It is not offered to the public and does not let other people sign in.

2. Information we collect

The App does not collect, store or process personal data of third-party users. The only data it handles is:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts, issued by TikTok and Google when the owner authorizes the App.
  • Basic account information returned by those APIs for the owner's own accounts (for example, account ID or display name), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video files, captions and post settings that the owner chooses to publish.

3. How information is used

This information is used only to authenticate with TikTok and YouTube and to publish the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is not sold.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are not sent to any server operated by us. This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and the callback page does not transmit any data. You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok or Google account settings.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok and Google, as needed to publish content through their APIs. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing. We do not share data with anyone else.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when access is revoked or the App is no longer used. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email us at the address above.

7. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data relating to you by contacting us. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
